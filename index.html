How it works

No team wallet, no manual buybacks. The wall is just the creator fees, and it only ever spends at the floor.

01 · STACK

Fees become stone

Every $BEDROCK trade pays a creator fee in to the launch wallet. All of it stays there as the wall.

02 · SET

The floor follows the high

The floor is of the highest 5-minute close in the last 6 hours. A new high lifts the wall with it.

03 · HOLD

Dips get bought and burned

Price touches the floor: the wall buys with of itself, burns the $BEDROCK, waits 30s, and repeats while price stays there.

Floor = 0.70 × max(5m close, last 6h) · Buy when price ≤ floor · Chunk = 20% of the wall, max 5% price impact, every 30s · Everything bought is burned.

Rules

  • The wall is the balance of the launch wallet. Nothing else is in it, and nothing leaves it except buys at the floor.
  • Floor = of the highest 5-minute close over the last 6 hours. Price from Jupiter, candles from GeckoTerminal.
  • At or under the floor the wall buys of itself every 30 seconds, never more than 5% price impact per buy.
  • Every $BEDROCK the wall buys is burned, in a transaction carrying the memo BEDROCK-HOLD.
  • Above the floor the wall does nothing. It just keeps growing with every trade.
  • The engine posts a BEDROCK-WALL heartbeat every 30 min. Launch wallet:
